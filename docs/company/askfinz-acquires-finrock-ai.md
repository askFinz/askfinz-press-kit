# askFinz acquires FinRock.ai for financial data and insight

askFinz has acquired FinRock.ai, the AI-driven investment research platform behind "Ask Finn", bringing its predictive market analytics, sentiment tracking and regulatory document analysis into the askFinz Finance workspace.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/press/askfinz-acquires-finrock-ai](https://askfinz.com/press/askfinz-acquires-finrock-ai)

## Key points

### Why this acquisition

Financial questions are rarely answered by a single document. They are answered by a filing read next to the call that explained it, the sentiment around it, and the history that puts it in context.

### What FinRock.ai brings

FinRock.ai entered a closed alpha release ahead of the transaction, and that programme continues. Existing alpha participants keep their access, and their feedback now feeds directly into the Finance workspace roadmap.

### What changes for askFinz users

FinRock's analytics are being folded into finance.askfinz.ai, the askFinz Finance workspace, rather than kept as a separate subscription. The intent is that a question asked in Finance draws on three things at once:

### Availability

FinRock capabilities are being introduced into the askFinz Finance workspace on a rolling basis. The Finance workspace is available on the askFinz platform; details are at askfinz.com/apps/finance and askfinz.com/pricing.
