### Principles we hold ourselves to

We measure ourselves against craft instruments — a great editor, a great scope, a great kitchen knife — not against demos.

### Where your data lives

askFinz is distributed by design. Pages are served from the nearest of 300+ edge cities so they feel local wherever you open them, while your files, code and backups are homed in European data centers.
