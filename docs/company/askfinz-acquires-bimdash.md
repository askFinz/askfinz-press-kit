# askFinz acquires BIMDash, a 3D model viewer for Estate

askFinz has acquired BIMDash, the cloud-based BIM model checker, and will integrate its 3D viewer into askFinz Estate — putting the model beside the listing and the planning record it belongs to.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/press/askfinz-acquires-bimdash](https://askfinz.com/press/askfinz-acquires-bimdash)

## Key points

### A direct line from Verifi3D

BIMDash did not begin from a blank page.

### Why askFinz wanted it

askFinz already reads the property market. Its property and construction collection currently holds around 709,000 listings — read from agents, portals and public planning records — and grew by roughly 7.3% in the last 24 hours at the time of writing.

### Status, stated plainly

askFinz Estate has not launched. It is listed as forthcoming on askfinz.com/apps, and estate.askfinz.ai is a placeholder today.

### Availability

askFinz Estate will be announced when it ships. Progress is tracked at askfinz.com/apps/estate, and the underlying collection is already searchable at askfinz.com/collections/property-and-construction.
